How Collab works

  1. Upload your track below (a beat, a rough mix, or a finished song).
  2. Tap Create invite link and send it to a friend.
  3. They open it on their phone, hear your track, and record a layer over it. No app or account needed.
  4. You get an email. Accept or mute each layer, adjust volume and timing, then download the mix.
