How Gemstar Works

A quick rundown of the basics — every knob also shows this same text if you press and hold it (or hover, on desktop).

  • Record straight from your mic or upload a file — either way, Gemstar processes it right in your browser, nothing to install.
  • Use the A/B button to flip between your raw vocal ("Before") and the processed version ("After") so you can hear exactly what the knobs are doing.
  • Try a Preset from the dropdown for a quick starting point, or start from scratch — every knob starts at 0 until you move it.
  • When you're happy with it, Export MP3 downloads a real MP3 encoded right in your browser.

Help

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most.

What is Gemstar?

A vocal processing studio that runs right in your browser, built by engineers M5tar and Jewels. Upload or record a vocal, dial in the knobs or pick a preset, then export an MP3 or 24-bit WAV.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Everything runs live in your browser on your own device, and your audio is never uploaded to a server. An up-to-date Chrome, Safari, Edge or Firefox works best.

Can I record through my audio interface?

Yes, on Mix and Unlimited. Plug it in, tap Record Vocal, and the input picker under the timer auto-selects your interface. If it still records from the computer mic, choose the interface in that dropdown and allow microphone access for the site. On Starter, Gemstar records from your computer's default input, so set your interface as the input in your computer's sound settings.

Can I load more than one vocal?

On Mix (3 tracks) and Unlimited (8 tracks) you can drop several vocal files at once. Tap ☆ Make Lead to choose your lead, use 🎛 FX to give each vocal its own full effects chain, and set pan, mute, solo or a one-tap role (Background, Double, Ad-lib) on every track.

How do I play just one part of the song?

Tap the timeline under the waveform to choose where playback starts, or drag across it to select a section. Dragging turns Loop on, so that section repeats while you dial in the knobs.

Why does my vocal sound robotic?

That's the Speed (Tune) knob, which is high in the Auto-Tuned preset. Turn Speed down for a gentler glide and raise Humanize for a more natural sound.

I press Play but hear nothing.

Check your volume and that the browser tab isn't muted, then reload. If you use an audio interface, make sure your computer's sound output is set to what you're listening on. Still stuck? Email Gemstaraudio@gmail.com.

What do the plans include?

Starter ($19/mo): the full core vocal chain, 1 vocal track. Mix ($49/mo): Full Song Mix with your beat, the M5tar & Jewels signature knobs, and 3 vocal tracks with their own effects chains. Unlimited ($99/mo): everything in Mix, 8 vocal tracks, unlimited songs.

Does Gemstar remember my settings?

Yes, in the same browser. Your knobs, output gain, Auto-Tune key and language are saved automatically. Audio files aren't stored, but drop the same files again and each one picks its level, pan and knob settings back up.
