How Gemstar Works

A quick rundown of the basics — every knob also shows this same text if you press and hold it (or hover, on desktop).

  • Record straight from your mic or upload a file — either way, Gemstar processes it right in your browser, nothing to install.
  • Use the A/B button to flip between your raw vocal ("Before") and the processed version ("After") so you can hear exactly what the knobs are doing.
  • Try a Preset from the dropdown for a quick starting point, or start from scratch — every knob starts at 0 until you move it.
  • When you're happy with it, Export MP3 downloads a real MP3 encoded right in your browser.
